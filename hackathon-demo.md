# ShieldSense Hackathon Demo Flow

The original ShieldSense landing page remains available at `/`. The dedicated judge-facing demo is available at `/demo` and is intentionally designed for a short, reliable presentation.

| Step | Interaction | Proof point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select one of three message cases. | The evaluation begins with a plausible, ordinary-looking request. |
| 2 | Press **Analyze message**. | The signal field enters an explicit reading state instead of showing an unexplained result. |
| 3 | Inspect the completed two-channel read. | Technical indicators and human-manipulation indicators appear beside one another. |
| 4 | Read the recommended action. | The experience ends with a clear, practical response rather than a raw risk score. |

The cases are clearly labeled as **hackathon demonstration cases**, not customer messages or production detections. They are designed to demonstrate product interaction, not imply a live scanning result.

## 45-second demo narration

> “This message looks routine: it has a familiar business name, a practical request, and a link. I select it and ask ShieldSense to read both channels. In one read, ShieldSense finds technical evidence—the recent domain and redirect chain—and human evidence—the artificial deadline and impersonated identity. Instead of returning an unexplained score, it tells the user what to do next: do not open the link, and verify through a known channel. The proof panel shows the product path: extension event, two-channel evidence split, then an explainable response.”

## Deliberately deferred after the hackathon

The hackathon build does not attempt account history, persistent user scan logs, custom team policy controls, recipient notification workflows, or a broad marketing-site expansion. These are valuable follow-on features, but the current scope keeps the live demonstration focused on a single, explainable decision.
